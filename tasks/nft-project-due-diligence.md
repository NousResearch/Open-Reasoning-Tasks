# NFT Project Due Diligence

## Description:
Analyze NFT projects to assess legitimacy, value proposition, and investment potential. This task requires evaluating team credibility, tokenomics, roadmap viability, community strength, technical implementation, and identifying red flags vs. green flags before making mint/purchase decisions.

## Modality:
Text -> Text

## Examples:

### Example 1:

Input:

```
NFT Project Analysis Request

Project: "CyberPunks 3D" (fictional)

Project Info:
- Supply: 10,000 NFTs
- Mint Price: 0.08 ETH
- Mint Date: 7 days from now
- Blockchain: Ethereum

Team:
- Anonymous founders
- Claims to have "ex-Google engineers"
- No LinkedIn profiles or verifiable credentials
- Active on Twitter (5,000 followers, account 2 months old)

Roadmap:
- Phase 1: Mint completion
- Phase 2: Metaverse integration (partner TBA)
- Phase 3: Token airdrop for holders
- Phase 4: P2E game development

Utility:
- "Exclusive metaverse access"
- "Future airdrops"
- "Community DAO voting"

Art:
- 3D rendered punks with various traits
- No documented rarity breakdown
- Sample images look AI-generated

Community:
- Discord: 12,000 members (mostly joined in last week)
- Twitter: Moderate engagement
- Reddit: No organic discussion

Contract:
- Not verified yet
- "Will be revealed at mint"

Question: Should I mint? What are the risks?
```

Output:

```
ANALYSIS: HIGH RISK - DO NOT MINT

🚩 CRITICAL RED FLAGS (Deal Breakers)

1. Anonymous Team with Unverifiable Claims
   - "Ex-Google engineers" = unverifiable marketing
   - No doxxing, no LinkedIn, no prior projects
   - RISK: Rug pull likelihood high
   - Score: 0/10

2. Contract Not Verified Pre-Mint
   - Hidden contract = could contain malicious code
   - No way to verify total supply, mint mechanics, royalties
   - Could have hidden mint functions for team
   - RISK: Technical exploit/unfair advantages
   - Score: 0/10

3. Suspicious Community Growth
   - 12k Discord in 1 week = likely botted
   - No organic Reddit discussion = community not real
   - RISK: Fake hype, few real buyers
   - Score: 1/10

4. Vague Roadmap Without Specifics
   - "Partner TBA" = no actual partnerships
   - "Metaverse integration" = meaningless without details
   - "P2E game" = vaporware 99% of the time
   - RISK: Promises won't materialize
   - Score: 2/10

⚠️ MODERATE RED FLAGS

5. AI-Generated Art
   - Not inherently bad, but suggests low effort
   - No unique artistic vision
   - RISK: Commoditized, no lasting value
   - Score: 3/10

6. Recent Twitter Account
   - 2 months old = project rushed
   - Limited track record
   - RISK: Fly-by-night operation
   - Score: 3/10

7. Utility Claims Too Generic
   - Every project promises "exclusive metaverse access"
   - DAO voting without DAO structure = BS
   - Token airdrop = vague promise
   - RISK: No real utility
   - Score: 2/10

LEGITIMACY ASSESSMENT

Team Transparency: 0/10
Technical Execution: 0/10
Community Authenticity: 1/10
Roadmap Credibility: 2/10
Art/Innovation: 3/10
Utility/Value Prop: 2/10

OVERALL SCORE: 8/60 = 13% (EXTREMELY LOW)

EXPECTED SCENARIO

Most Likely Outcome (70% probability):
- Team mints out or partially sells
- Delivers nothing from roadmap
- Discord becomes inactive within weeks
- Floor price crashes 80-95%
- Team disappears

Secondary Outcome (25% probability):
- Partial delivery on minor items
- Project slowly dies due to lack of real value
- Small community of bagholders

Best Case (5% probability):
- Surprise execution on roadmap
- Still probably a bad investment at 0.08 ETH

FINANCIAL ANALYSIS

Investment: 0.08 ETH × 10,000 = 800 ETH total raise
That's ~$1.3M at current prices

Where does this money go with anon team?
- RISK: Straight to founders' wallets, then cash out

Expected Value Calculation:
- 70% chance of 90% loss = -0.072 ETH
- 25% chance of 50% loss = -0.010 ETH
- 5% chance of 2x gain = +0.004 ETH
- Expected Value: -0.078 ETH (97% loss expectation)

RECOMMENDATION: HARD PASS

Do not mint this project. It exhibits textbook rug pull characteristics:
1. Anonymous team with fake credentials
2. Hidden contract
3. Fake community metrics
4. Vague, unrealistic promises
5. Cash grab mint price for low-effort art

Better alternatives:
- Established blue chip NFTs (BAYC, Azuki, etc.) if you want exposure
- Projects with doxxed teams and verified contracts
- Collections with actual utility already delivered
- Waiting for post-mint floor if you must have exposure

If you want to speculate on new projects, look for:
✅ Doxxed team with track record
✅ Contract verified before mint
✅ Organic community growth
✅ Specific, achievable roadmap
✅ Unique art/value proposition
✅ Reasonable mint price

This project has ZERO of these qualities.

VERDICT: 🚫 DO NOT MINT - 95%+ chance of loss
```

### Example 2:

Input:

```
Evaluate established NFT project for purchase

Project: "Doodles" (real project)

Current Stats:
- Floor Price: 2.5 ETH
- Total Volume: 90,000 ETH
- Holders: 5,200
- Supply: 10,000

Team:
- Doxxed founders (Burnt Toast - known artist)
- Professional team with prior success
- Regular updates and communication

Delivered Utility:
- Multiple IRL events (SV, NFT NYC)
- Doodles 2 (customizable avatars)
- Brand partnerships (Crocs, others)
- Animation studio acquisition
- Merchandise line

Community:
- Strong, engaged holders
- Active Discord with real discussions
- Recognized brand in NFT space

Financials:
- Floor held relatively well vs market
- Consistent trading volume
- Royalties funding ongoing development

Question: Is 2.5 ETH floor a good entry point compared to peak of 8 ETH?
```

Output:

```
ANALYSIS: MODERATE-QUALITY PROJECT, QUESTIONABLE ENTRY

✅ GREEN FLAGS (Strengths)

1. Credible, Doxxed Team
   - Burnt Toast = established artist with following
   - Team delivered on promises (rare in NFT space)
   - Professional operations
   - Score: 9/10

2. Real Utility Delivered
   - IRL events provide tangible value
   - Doodles 2 shows technical capability
   - Brand partnerships = revenue potential
   - Not just JPEGs
   - Score: 8/10

3. Sustainable Community
   - 5,200 holders = decent distribution
   - Organic engagement (not botted)
   - Long-term believers still holding
   - Score: 8/10

4. Brand Recognition
   - One of recognized "blue chip" collections
   - Mainstream awareness (Crocs collab, etc.)
   - Score: 7/10

⚠️ CONCERNS & RISKS

1. Down 69% from ATH
   - Floor was 8 ETH, now 2.5 ETH
   - Question: Is this "discount" or "fair value"?
   - Context matters: Entire NFT market down 70-80%

2. NFT Market Macro
   - Overall interest has waned significantly
   - Trading volumes down across board
   - Speculative mania over
   - RISK: Further downside if market continues decline

3. Utility Value vs Price
   - IRL events = nice but limited economic value
   - Doodles 2 = additional NFTs, dilutes value?
   - Brand partnerships = unclear revenue to holders
   - Question: Does utility justify 2.5 ETH ($4,000)?

4. Opportunity Cost
   - 2.5 ETH could buy other assets
   - NFT liquidity is poor (hard to exit fast)
   - Better risk/reward elsewhere?

COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

Doodles vs Alternatives:

Blue Chips:
- BAYC: ~30 ETH (12x Doodles)
- Azuki: ~5 ETH (2x Doodles)
- Clone X: ~1.2 ETH (0.5x Doodles)

Doodles pricing seems middle-tier among blue chips.

vs ETH/BTC:
- 2.5 ETH could stay liquid in ETH
- Potential upside if ETH rallies
- No liquidity lock-up

vs Other Collectibles:
- Could buy physical art, watches, etc.
- More established markets
- Easier to value

SCENARIOS & PROBABILITIES

Bear Case (40% probability):
- NFT market stays depressed 12-24 months
- Floor drifts to 1.5-2 ETH
- Locked capital, limited exit liquidity
- Loss: -20% to -40%

Base Case (35% probability):
- Floor stabilizes around 2-3 ETH
- Project continues delivering utility
- Decent but not explosive returns
- Return: -10% to +20%

Bull Case (20% probability):
- NFT market revival
- Doodles brand value increases
- Major partnership or development
- Return: +50% to +100% (back to 4-5 ETH)

Moon Case (5% probability):
- Full market cycle returns
- Doodles hits new ATH
- Return: +200%+ (back to 8+ ETH)

Expected Value:
- 40% × -30% = -12%
- 35% × +5% = +1.75%
- 20% × +75% = +15%
- 5% × +200% = +10%
- EV = +14.75% (marginal positive)

RECOMMENDATION: CAUTIOUSLY POSITIVE, BUT...

If you buy at 2.5 ETH:

✅ DO IT IF:
- You value the community and events
- You have conviction in NFT market recovery
- You can afford to hold 12-24 months
- You're okay with illiquidity
- You want established brand exposure
- You have strong hands (won't panic sell)

🚫 DON'T DO IT IF:
- You need liquidity soon
- You're hoping for quick flip
- You don't care about community/utility
- You'd be overexposed to NFTs
- You'd panic sell if floor goes to 1.5 ETH
- You're investing with rent money

ALTERNATIVE STRATEGIES

1. Wait & Watch
   - See if floor breaks below 2 ETH
   - Better risk/reward at 1.5 ETH
   - Patience often rewarded in NFTs

2. DCA Approach
   - Buy 0.5 now, 0.5 at 2 ETH, 0.5 at 1.5 ETH
   - Averages down if it drops
   - Reduces timing risk

3. Allocate % of Crypto Portfolio
   - If you have 10 ETH, 1-2 ETH in Doodles = reasonable
   - If you have 3 ETH total, 2.5 ETH in Doodles = too concentrated

FINAL VERDICT

Quality Project: 8/10
Current Value: 6/10 (not cheap, but not expensive)
Risk/Reward: 6/10 (moderate)
Entry Timing: 5/10 (could wait for better price)

DECISION: CAUTIOUSLY BUY if:
- You're bullish on NFT market recovery
- You value the community and events
- You can hold through volatility
- It's <20% of your crypto portfolio

Otherwise, WAIT for:
- Further floor decline to 1.5-2 ETH
- Signs of NFT market recovery
- Major positive catalyst for project

This is a "hold your nose and buy quality during fear" situation - if you believe in the category and can stomach volatility.
```
